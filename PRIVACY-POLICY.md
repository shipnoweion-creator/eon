## 1. Overview and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of Eon ("Eon," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, stores, and deletes personal information when you use the Eon AI assistant application, website, support channels, or related services (collectively, the "Service"). It also explains how information is handled when you connect an Instagram professional account through the Instagram Graph API.

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service or connect an Instagram account. This policy does not replace Meta's own privacy terms or the privacy practices of other third-party services.

> **Plain-language summary**
> We request only the permissions needed for enabled features, use connected data to perform the actions you request, apply safeguards to protect it, and give you ways to disconnect Instagram and request deletion. We do not sell personal information.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide directly

- Account information, such as your name, email address, organization name, and login or support details.
- Assistant inputs, including prompts, instructions, draft captions, scheduling choices, uploaded files, and other content you submit.
- Communications you send to us, including support requests, feedback, and deletion requests.
- Preferences and settings, such as connected accounts, enabled features, language, and notification choices.

### 2.2 Information received through the Instagram Graph API

When you connect Instagram, Meta provides information permitted by the access token, the permissions you approve, the type of Instagram account you use, and the features enabled in Eon. Depending on those choices, this may include:

- Instagram account identifiers and profile information, including an account ID, username, profile name, profile picture, biography, website, and professional-account metadata.
- Connected Facebook Page identifiers and relationship information required to access an Instagram professional account.
- Media and publishing information, including images or videos, captions, media identifiers, permalinks, timestamps, publishing status, and content you ask Eon to create, retrieve, schedule, or publish.
- Engagement information, such as comments, mentions, replies, and related account or media identifiers, when an enabled feature and approved permission require it.
- Insights and analytics, such as reach, impressions, engagement, follower trends, and other metrics made available by Meta for the connected account or media.
- Instagram messaging information only if messaging features are enabled and separately authorized, which may include message content, sender or recipient identifiers, timestamps, and attachments.

Eon does not automatically receive your Instagram password. Authentication is handled through Meta's authorization process. We receive an access token or similar credential that allows only the approved access. The specific permissions requested should be shown during connection and may change as you enable or disable features.

### 2.3 Automatically collected technical information

- Device and browser information, such as device type, operating system, browser type, language, and approximate region derived from an Internet Protocol address.
- Usage and diagnostic data, such as feature interactions, request times, error reports, performance information, and security events.
- Cookie or similar technology data where used by the Service. A separate cookie notice or consent control will apply where required.

## 3. How we use and handle information

We process information only for defined purposes and apply access limits appropriate to each purpose. Depending on the feature, our legal basis may be performance of a contract, consent, legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, or compliance with law.

### 3.1 Providing the Service

- Authenticate you, connect the Instagram account you select, and maintain the connection.
- Read, organize, summarize, analyze, draft, schedule, publish, or manage Instagram content only as needed for the feature you request.
- Generate AI-assisted outputs from your prompts and authorized Instagram context, such as caption drafts, content ideas, suggested replies, or performance summaries.
- Display account information, media, comments, messages, or insights in the Service when the relevant feature is enabled.
- Remember settings, process support requests, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

### 3.2 AI processing

To generate a response, the Service may send the minimum necessary prompt, context, or connected content to an artificial intelligence model provider acting as a service provider. The provider is permitted to process that information only under the applicable service agreement and our instructions. Eon should not use Instagram user data to train a general-purpose model unless the user gives separate, informed consent and the practice is permitted by Meta's terms and applicable law.

AI outputs may be inaccurate or inappropriate. You are responsible for reviewing drafts before use unless you have deliberately enabled an automated action. If automation is enabled, Eon will use the scope, safeguards, and account permissions configured for that feature. Content you direct Eon to publish becomes visible according to the selected Instagram audience and may be copied or shared by others.

### 3.3 Purpose limitations

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Instagram Graph API data for unrelated advertising, create sensitive profiles from connected content, or attempt to access information beyond the permissions and features you authorize. We do not make high-impact decisions about employment, housing, credit, insurance, education, or legal eligibility based on connected Instagram data.

## 4. When information is shared

We may disclose information only in the circumstances below and only to the extent reasonably necessary:

- **Service providers.** Hosting, database, security, analytics, customer-support, communications, and AI model providers may process information on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
- **Meta and Instagram.** Requests, content, and instructions are transmitted to Meta when necessary to authenticate an account or perform an Instagram action. Meta processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy.
- **At your direction.** We share information with recipients or services you select, including when you publish content or ask the Service to interact with an Instagram user.
- **Legal and safety reasons.** We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce terms, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, or respond to valid legal process.
- **Business transactions.** Information may transfer in a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice where required.

## 5. Data security measures

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Measures should include:

- Encryption in transit using current transport security protocols and encryption at rest for sensitive stored data where appropriate.
- Role-based access controls, least-privilege permissions, multifactor authentication for administrative systems, and periodic access reviews.
- Secure handling of Meta access tokens and other credentials, including restricted storage, rotation where supported, and revocation when no longer needed.
- Logging, monitoring, vulnerability management, software updates, backups, incident-response procedures, and vendor security reviews.
- Staff or contractor confidentiality obligations and privacy or security training appropriate to their access.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will investigate and provide notice when required by applicable law.

## 7. How to disconnect Instagram or delete data

### 7.1 Disconnect Instagram

1. **Open Eon settings.** Go to Connected Accounts or Integrations.
2. **Select Instagram.** Choose Disconnect or Revoke Access and confirm.
3. **Review access at Meta.** You may also remove the Eon integration through the relevant Facebook or Instagram business-integration settings associated with your account.

Disconnecting stops future access through that connection but does not necessarily delete previously stored data. To delete stored information, follow the request process below.

### 7.2 Request deletion

1. **Use the in-app control.** Open Settings, choose Privacy or Data Controls, select Delete My Data or Delete Account, and confirm the request, if that control is available.
2. **Or contact us.** Send a request to the privacy email listed in Section 13 with the subject "Data Deletion Request." Identify the Eon account and connected Instagram username, but do not send your password or access token.
3. **Complete verification.** We may ask for information reasonably necessary to confirm that you control the account. We will not request your Instagram password.
4. **Receive confirmation.** We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, unless law permits or requires a longer period. We will confirm completion or explain any lawful exception.

Deletion removes or de-identifies covered data from active systems. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups until they cycle out, ordinarily within 90 days, and records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal claims, or compliance. During that period, backup data is isolated from ordinary use.

## 8. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, obtain a copy of, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent, opt out of certain processing, or appeal a denied request. These rights may be subject to legal exceptions.

- **Instagram permissions:** disconnect the account or revoke the integration through Meta's settings at any time.
- **AI history:** delete individual conversations or request deletion of your account data where the Service supports those controls.
- **Marketing:** use the unsubscribe link in an email or update notification settings. Service and security notices may still be sent.
- **Complaints:** contact us first so we can address the concern. You may also contact the privacy or data-protection authority where you live, when applicable.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right. Authorized agents may submit a request where local law permits, subject to verification of authority and identity.
