# Data Deletion Instructions for Eon

**Effective date:** September 26, 2026

This page explains how to disconnect your Instagram account from **Eon** and request deletion of data associated with your use of Eon’s Instagram Graph API integration.

You do not need to provide your Instagram password, Meta password, access token, or any other authentication secret to request deletion.

---

## Option 1: Disconnect Eon

You can stop future Instagram Graph API access by disconnecting Eon in the application, if that control is available, or by removing Eon from the connected-app or business-integration settings provided by Meta or Instagram.

Because Meta may change the names and locations of these controls, use the account settings for the Instagram or Meta account you originally connected and look for **Apps and websites**, **Business integrations**, or a similar connected-apps section. Select Eon and remove or revoke its access.

Disconnecting Eon stops future API access after the revocation takes effect. It does not necessarily delete information already retained by Eon, so complete Option 2 if you also want stored data deleted.

## Option 2: Request deletion from Eon

Send a deletion request through the privacy contact method listed in Eon’s Privacy Policy or through the support contact shown in the application listing.

Include only the information needed to locate your account:

- **Request type:** State that you are requesting deletion of Eon data.
- **Connected account:** Provide the Instagram username connected to Eon or the app-scoped user ID, if available.
- **Contact address:** Use an email address or other contact method that can be used to verify and confirm the request.
- **Scope:** State whether you want all Eon data deleted or only specified information.

Do **not** include your password, access token, payment information, government ID, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## What happens next

1. **Verification:** We may ask for limited information to confirm that you control the account or contact address associated with the request.
2. **Processing:** After verification, we will delete or de-identify data that Eon controls and that can reasonably be linked to your account, subject to the exceptions below.
3. **Confirmation:** We will confirm completion through the contact method associated with the request when reasonably possible.

Deletion requests are handled without undue delay and within any period required by applicable law. If more time is legally permitted and reasonably necessary, we will provide notice where required.

## Requests initiated through Meta

If you initiate deletion through a Meta-provided flow, Eon may receive the request through its configured data-deletion callback. Eon will process the request and, where supported, provide a confirmation code or status information through that flow.

---

## Data covered by deletion

Depending on how you used Eon and which permissions you granted, deletion may cover:

- Instagram account identifiers and profile information received through the Instagram Graph API;
- Instagram media, captions, comments, messages, insights, or related metadata that Eon retained to provide requested features;
- Stored prompts, instructions, generated content, preferences, and connection records associated with your account; and
- Access tokens or authorization records still controlled by Eon.

The exact data available for deletion depends on the features you used and the data Eon retained under its Privacy Policy.

## Limited exceptions

Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful request;
- Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims;
- Detect or prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents;
- Maintain records of consent, deletion, or account disconnection; or
- Complete deletion from backups during the ordinary backup-rotation cycle.

Information retained for these purposes will be limited, protected, and used only for the applicable purpose. Aggregated or de-identified information that can no longer reasonably identify you may also be retained where permitted by law.

## If you cannot access your connected account

Use the privacy or support contact listed in Eon’s Privacy Policy or application listing. Explain that you cannot access the connected account and provide only the minimum information needed to identify the connection. Additional verification may be required before account-linked data can be deleted.

## Contact

For deletion requests or questions, use the privacy contact method identified in Eon’s Privacy Policy or the support contact shown in the application listing.
